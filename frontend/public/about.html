A note from Christina

I started writing to clear my head, not because I had a plan.

I write for the kids who feel everything a little too much — the ones who replay conversations on the walk home, who notice faces in a crowded room, who carry things quietly. I was one of those kids. I’m raising five of them now.

Our house is loud, full, and occasionally overwhelming, which means it’s also very good material. Writing is how I process. I’m a granddaughter, a daughter, a wife, a mother, and a reader, in roughly that order on a good day.

I love being outside. I do not love being told what kind of author I should be. The books I make are the ones I needed when I was a kid: honest, a little messy, unwilling to tie things up too neatly.

If you have ever sat with a feeling you could not name, this shelf is for you.
