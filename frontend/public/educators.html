Why these books

Honest stories help children name what they are already feeling.

The middle-grade and YA titles on this shelf were written for the children who notice everything — and for the educators and parents who hold space for them. They are quiet on the surface and busy underneath, and they reward slow, classroom-paced reading.

Below you’ll find reading levels, curriculum connections, discussion questions, and read-aloud notes for every available title. School visits, virtual author chats and book clubs welcome — please use the form on the contact page.

In the classroom

A few of the curriculum connections.

Reading levels

Lexile, Fountas & Pinnell and grade-equivalent bands available per title. Useful for differentiated classroom libraries.

Curriculum links

Social-emotional learning, identity, family, and the language of emotion. Pairs well with poetry units and writers’ workshops.

Read-aloud notes

Suggestions for pacing, pauses, and the small moments worth lingering on. Designed for teachers, librarians and parents.
