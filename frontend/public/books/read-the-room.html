Read the Room.

About the book

A novel about the small, exhausting work of being the one who notices first.

June can read a room before she steps into it. She has been the steady one, the careful one, the one her family relies on to soften the edges. In her last year of secondary school, she begins to wonder what it would mean to be the one who is read — instead of the one who is reading.

For the teenagers who carry quiet weight, and for the people who love them. Honest, a little messy, and unwilling to tie things up too neatly.

Themes
  • Emotional labour
  • Family & obligation
  • Friendship
  • First love
  • Becoming visible
Strong fit for upper secondary school programmes on identity, mental health and the social-emotional curriculum. Discussion guide forthcoming.
